Play in Unciv

The $product map of Spain can be played in Unciv, the free and open-source Civilization V for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android. It has $width × $height tiles of $tile_km km: the peninsula, the Balearic Islands, the Canary Islands (in their inset, as on the map), Ceuta, Melilla and the neighbouring land of Portugal, France and North Africa. Each autonomous community is a civilization that starts in its capital, with its flag and its municipalities, and Ceuta and Melilla are city-states.

Unciv mod ($zip_name) Map only

On a computer (Windows, macOS, Linux)

  1. Install Unciv as its page says: on Windows, the Unciv.msi installer; on Linux, Flathub or itch.io; on macOS, brew install unciv.
  2. Open Unciv and go to Mods. Press Download mod from URL, paste $url/$zip_href and press Download.
  3. Back in the menu, press Start new game. Under Map Type choose Custom and, under Map file, $map_name. The game turns on the $mod_name mod by itself (you will see it ticked under Extension mods): it brings the autonomous communities, Ceuta, Melilla and the Iberian pig.
  4. Play as your community: press your player's flag (the Human one) and pick yours. The rivals you add with the + are communities at random, each in its capital.
  5. Press Start game!.

All $n_nations at once: press Select players from starting locations. They all join and you get one at random; for another, remove with the − the AI player that has it and pick it on your flag. Spain is still in the list of civilizations, with no place on the map: if you pick it, the game finds it one.

Without the mod: download the map only and save it, named $map_name and without an extension, in the maps folder of Unciv's data (if you run it with java -jar Unciv.jar, that is the folder of the .jar). It shows under Map file and in the map editor. It is the same map without the Iberian pig or the communities, which only exist with the mod: on it Spain starts in Madrid and the other players in the largest AUDES urban areas, at least $gap_km km apart ($starts).

On Android

  1. Install Unciv from Google Play or F-Droid.
  2. Open Unciv, go to Mods, press Download mod from URL, paste $url/$zip_href and press Download.
  3. Carry on from step 3 above. Android does not need the separate map: the mod has it inside.

From Unciv's mod browser

The mod is also on GitHub ($mod_repo), so it is listed in the game's mods: go to Mods, look for Spainciv under Downloadable mods (or in the Maps category), pick it and press Download. Updates arrive the same way.

Forest, grassland, plains, marshReal: CORINE Land Cover 2018. Forest where forest covers $forest_pct% or more; grassland where meadows, pastures and wetlands outweigh crops and scrub; marsh where wetlands pass $marsh_pct%. The rest is plains.
TundraConvention: with no climate data, height stands in for cold: land at a mean elevation of $tundra_m m or more is tundra.

RiversReal: the rivers of the $product map (Natural Earth), moved to the tile edges, as Unciv draws them ($river_edges segments).
Rock of GibraltarThe only Unciv natural wonder that really is on this map, on the tile of Gibraltar.
Communities and capitalsReal: the name and the capital or seat of government that each one's Statute of Autonomy gives (or the law it refers to), with the other official name in brackets. Canarias has two capitals and its Presidency alternates between them: it starts in the larger, Las Palmas de Gran Canaria. Unciv lets no city be founded $city_gap tiles or less from another, so the less populous community of each pair too close together starts on the nearest free tile of its land (tiles from its capital: $moved).
CitiesReal: its $city_names most populous municipalities in the $padron_year Padrón (INE), the capital first, with the IGN's official name. Where each city is founded is up to the game: the fifth city you found takes the fifth municipality's name, wherever it is.
LeaderThe office the Statute names ("President of the Junta of Andalucía"), never a person.
Flags and coloursReal: the design the Statute describes, simplified and without the arms of Aragón, La Rioja, Ceuta and Melilla (Navarra keeps the chains, without the crown and the emerald). Convention: the exact shade of each colour (only Asturias fixes it in a law) and which two are each civilization's in the game, which Unciv wants to contrast.
AdvantageReal: the map resource of which the community holds the most tiles in its land and coast relative to its size (among those it holds $unique_min or more tiles of). Convention: what it gives, one more food, production and gold on each tile of that resource its cities work.

Resources

Each resource comes from data. Minerals go to the tile of their deposit or their mine: a Civilization resource is a deposit, not a working mine, so the large and medium deposits of IGME's BDMIN count even if they are no longer worked (the coal of León, Palencia or Asturias, the uranium of Salamanca or Jaén). Products go to the tile with the largest area of their designation of origin, and horses to the comarcas with the most equines per km². Unciv only allows each resource on certain terrains (coal on plains, grassland, hills or forest; uranium on forest, tundra or marsh); if its tile does not qualify, it goes to a neighbouring one that does, and if none does it is left out. Uranium and aluminum, which fit very few terrains, look up to two tiles further, so their tile can be up to about 50 km from the deposit. There is one per tile, and several deposits of the same thing close together give one. The amounts of the strategic resources are those of a large Unciv deposit.

ResourceTilesWhere it comes from

Left without a tile: $not_placed. Nearly all the stone quarries fall on plains, where Unciv puts no stone, and nearly all the uranium on plains of Salamanca and Sierra Morena with no forest, tundra or marsh within 50 km. These designations found no free tile that takes them: $designations.

The Iberian pig is a luxury resource of this mod's own: Unciv has no pigs. It goes on grassland, plains or hills, is improved with a pasture and gives food and gold. It is a luxury and not a bonus resource (like cattle or sheep) because the four designations protect Iberian ham, a cured product, just as the wine designations protect wine and not grapes.
